Services

Engineering and project services

Supply is one part of the contract. We plan the system, install it, wire it and hand it over commissioned.

01 Project management

We plan the system, schedule the work and run it to handover. System planning, studio design layout and customer training are part of it.

02 Engineering services

Our engineers install and commission equipment on site, with years of field experience in Philippine broadcast behind them.
