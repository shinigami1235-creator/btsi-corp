What we carry

Five equipment groups covering the signal chain from the studio microphone to the antenna.

01RF transmission
01

RF transmission

Radio transmission runs from a 20W community FM exciter up to a 50kW AM plant. We carry Elenos FM transmitters and Continental Lensa AM transmitters, the remote control and changeover units that keep an unattended site running, and Aldena antennas to radiate the result.

18 products
02

TV transmission

Digital and analogue television transmission built around Itelco transmitters and MEX II exciters, with Tecsys headend equipment for receiving, converting and remultiplexing feeds, Linear Acoustic processors for loudness compliance, and Aldena antennas for Band I, Band III and UHF.

18 products
03

Playout systems

Automation that runs a channel without someone watching the clock. PlayBox covers television playout, ingest, on-screen graphics, ad insertion and archive, while WinMedia handles radio playout, music scheduling and traffic.

15 products
04

Studio and audio

A studio built on audio over IP. Axia consoles and xNodes carry the audio on the network, Omnia processors shape what goes to the transmitter, Telos systems put callers on air, Yellowtec mounts the microphones and Vicoustic panels treat the room.

51 products
05

IP codecs

Links that bring audio and video back from the field over whatever network is available. Telos codecs cover ISDN, IP and streaming links between studio and transmitter, and Comrex LiveShot sends two-way HD video over bonded cellular.

5 products
