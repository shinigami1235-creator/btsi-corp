Transmitter site

From a 20W exciter to a 50kW AM plant.

Elenos FM transmitters, Continental Lensa AM transmitters, the remote control and changeover units that keep an unattended site running, and Aldena antennas to radiate the result.

Studio

A studio built on audio over IP.

Axia consoles and xNodes carry the audio on the network, Omnia processors shape what goes to the transmitter, Telos systems put callers on air, Yellowtec mounts the microphones and Vicoustic panels treat the room.

Playout and headend

Automation that runs a channel without someone watching the clock.

PlayBox covers television playout, ingest, on-screen graphics, ad insertion and archive, while WinMedia handles radio playout, music scheduling and traffic.
